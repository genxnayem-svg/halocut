How it works

  1. Hold the trigger in Premiere Pro, After Effects or an app you added with + Add app (DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Photoshop, Lightroom…). The wheel opens where the pointer is.
  2. Move onto a segment: its tools fan out in an arc. Let go on the segment itself for its main tool (outlined).
  3. Flick out to a tool and let go. A short note by the pointer says what it did.
  4. Tap instead of hold and the wheel stays open: click a tool, or press Esc.

Make it yours

Every segment takes up to 12 tools, and the wheel up to 10 segments. Rename a segment by typing over its name; click its colour to change it.

Want a Premiere command that has no shortcut, like Nest or Scale to Frame Size? Give it one in Premiere Pro → Keyboard Shortcuts, then add it under Any shortcut.

Slice

How it works

  1. Hold the trigger: three rings open where the pointer is.
  2. Move out to a slice and let go: it runs in the app in front.
  3. Middle click steps the selection out to the next ring.
  4. Tap instead of hold to keep it open, then click. Esc or the centre cancels.

What a slice can do

Send keys, type text, open an app, file or link, or run an HaloCut tool in Premiere Pro or After Effects.

On a MacBook

F8 is a media key: press Fn + F8, or record another trigger such as a side button.

Every window, one wheel

  1. Hold the trigger anywhere. Each open app sits on the wheel with its icon; a badge shows how many windows it has.
  2. The app you used last is the first slice, straight up: flick up and let go to jump back, like ⌘ Tab.
  3. Rest on an app and its windows (or tabs) fan out. Let go on one to bring it to the front.
  4. Let go in the centre to stay where you are.

Button mapping

What each mouse button does in Premiere Pro. Other apps keep their own.

Taken over, not doubled

A button you give an action is taken over in that app: the app no longer sees it. Buttons set to "Nothing" work as before.

More buttons

Gaming and vertical mice have extra buttons. Press "Record another button" and press it; it gets its own row.

Devices

Any mouse your computer sees: wired, wireless or Bluetooth. No driver. Press each button once and it lights up.

Any mouse

What it never touches

HaloCut changes what your buttons do. It never changes your mouse's own settings: DPI, sensor, battery and lighting stay with the mouse and its maker's app.

    Moving to a new computer?

    Save your settings to a file here, then load it on the other computer. Wheels, buttons, favourites, triggers and looks all come along.

    Which size?

    Large is easier to hit on a big screen. Small keeps more of your timeline in sight while a wheel is open.

    Fewer notes

    Once you know the wheel by heart, switch the note off: the wheel closes the moment a tool runs.

    Setup & help

    Three steps, then it is in your editor.

    1
    Allow HaloCut to see the mouse and keyboard System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and Input Monitoring.
    2
    Install the Premiere Pro and After Effects bridge It starts by itself with each app; nothing to keep open.
    3
    Restart Premiere Pro or After Effects once The bridge connects within a few seconds. The optional panel is under Window › Extensions › HaloCut.

    Stuck?

    If the wheel doesn't open: switch HaloCut off and on again under Accessibility and Input Monitoring, then reopen it from the menu-bar icon.

    If a tool says the bridge is not running: restart that Adobe app once.
