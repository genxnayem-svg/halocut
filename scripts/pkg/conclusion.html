HaloCut is installed

  1. HaloCut has started: look for its icon in the menu bar.
  2. macOS will ask to let HaloCut use Accessibility and Input Monitoring. Turn both on in System Settings › Privacy & Security, so the wheels can follow your mouse.
  3. Open Premiere Pro or After Effects. The panels are under Window › Extensions › HaloCut.
